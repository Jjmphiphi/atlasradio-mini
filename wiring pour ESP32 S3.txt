Firmware already matches this map. Use **GPIO 1–13 only**. Do not use 0, 19/20 (USB), 26–37 (flash/PSRAM), 43/44 (UART), or 45/46 (strapping).

**MAX98357A (do this first when you want sound)**

| Amp | ESP32-S3 Super Mini |
|---|---|
| BCLK | GPIO **1** |
| LRC / WS | GPIO **2** |
| DIN | GPIO **4** |
| SD / SD_MODE | GPIO **7** |
| VIN | 5 V (amp side) |
| GND | GND |

GPIO **7** is mute: firmware drives it **LOW** to mute, and **high-Z + pull-up** to unmute. **Never wire that pin so the ESP is forced to output HIGH.** For one speaker, leave GAIN as the module default; firmware already downmixes to mono. Optional later: 470 kΩ from SD to 5 V for L+R mix on the chip itself.

You should already see `amp unmuted (GPIO 7 high-Z + pull-up)` at boot. After the amp is powered, Play 90s / SomaFM should make sound.
